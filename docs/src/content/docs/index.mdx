RATP Activity is an iPhone app. It shows the next metro and RER trains of the nearest Paris station on the lock screen, as a Live Activity.

These pages tell you, step by step, how to put the app on your iPhone. You need no experience with Xcode or with iOS apps.

<Aside>
  The tutorial is not written yet. Its pages will show in the sidebar, under "Tutorial".
</Aside>

## How to use the step lists

Each tutorial page has one list of steps. Tick a step when you have done it. The top of the list shows how many steps you have done. Your ticks stay in this browser, also when you close it.

Try it here:

<Checklist id="home-how-to">
  <Steps>

  1. Read the step to the end before you start it.

  2. Do the step.

  3. Tick the box of the step.

  </Steps>
</Checklist>
