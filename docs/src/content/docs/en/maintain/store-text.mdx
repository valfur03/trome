This page holds every text that you paste into App Store Connect and TestFlight for TROME 1.0, in French and in English, with the limit of its field and its size.
[The release guide](/en/maintain/release-on-the-app-store/) says where each field is and when to fill it in.
This page says what goes in it.

French comes first, because most riders are French: paste the French text in the primary language, then the English text.
Apple counts characters for every field below except the keywords and the review notes, which it counts in bytes: an accented letter such as é takes two bytes.
The limits are those of Apple's help pages, read on 7 October 2026: [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/) and [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/).
The sizes below are counted by script on the texts as they stand, so count again after any edit.

## The store title: three to try, in order

The title is the first thing a rider reads, and its limit is 30 characters.
The exact title `TROME` is probably refused: two apps already carry the word, a Peruvian newspaper and a hospital group in India, and App Store Connect refuses a name that another app holds.
So the title is `TROME`, a dash, and a few words that say what it is.
The name under the icon stays `TROME`, because it is another field that the store does not check.

Try the candidates in this order, and take the first that App Store Connect accepts.
Apple's public search (read on 7 October 2026) shows no app with these titles, and no transit app with the word.
It cannot see a name that an unreleased app record holds, so only App Store Connect settles it.

| Try | French (primary) | English (U.S.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TROME – Métro et RER Paris` (26) | `TROME – Paris Metro & RER` (25) |
| 2 | `TROME – Prochains trains Paris` (30) | `TROME – Next Paris Trains` (25) |
| 3 | `TROME – Prochains passages` (26) | `TROME – Next Metro & RER` (24) |

The first title says the two things that a Paris rider types in the search: the network and the city.
Other apps say the same in their titles, for example `Métro Paris & RER` and `Paris Metro, RER & Offline Map`, so no word of it belongs to one app, and the dash and `TROME` make the whole title yours.
The third title has no `RER` and no city: keep it for a refusal of a word.
The title never carries the name of RATP or of Île-de-France Mobilités: the source line at the end of the description is the only place for them.

| Notes | The English text below (2185 of 4000 bytes). Apple accepts any language, and English is the one that every reviewer reads. |

```text
I make TROME alone. It shows the next metro and RER trains of the station you walk into, as a Live Activity on the lock screen. It works in Paris and Île-de-France only. No account, no sign-in, no purchase, no ads. iPhone only, iOS 18 or later.

TO REVIEW FROM ANYWHERE (about 2 minutes, nothing to set up):
1. Open TROME. When iOS asks for your location, you may allow it or not: the app does not need it for the example.
2. On the main screen, tap "See an example". A Live Activity for Châtelet appears, with SAMPLE times made inside the app (no position, no network). Press the side button to lock the iPhone: it shows on the lock screen, and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one.
3. The sample times count down while the app runs. If iOS suspends the app and the lock screen says "Open the app", open TROME and tap "See an example" again. After 10 minutes, the example ends, at the latest when TROME opens again.
4. On the lock screen, tap the station name to see the next line. The buttons 30 min, 1 h and Today snooze the automatic mode (on the example, they only end it).
5. On the main screen: the Automatic mode switch, the Snooze row, the Location and Notifications rows, "Privacy policy" and "Help and feedback" (they open web pages in Safari).

HOW IT WORKS IN REAL USE (I recorded the attached video at a Paris station, on a real iPhone):
- Within about 150 meters of a metro or RER station, with the app open, the Live Activity starts with real times.
- With the app closed, iOS wakes TROME near a station. TROME sends a local notification, and one tap starts the Live Activity. This is why I ask for "Always" location: the app is closed when people arrive. With "While Using" only, the app still works while it is open.
- The position is used on the iPhone to find the nearest station. It is never sent to my server. The server gets only the identifier of a stop.
- Notifications are local, and optional.

TRAIN DATA: real times come from the PRIM service of Île-de-France Mobilités, through my server, which is running during the review. Metro and RER do not run all night: outside service hours there are no real times, but the example works at any hour.
```

Check each line of these notes against the build that you submit, with the rehearsal of the release guide.
Keep the sentence about the video only when the video is attached, and keep the sentence about the server only while it runs.

## What the text must not say

Each line below is a claim that the app cannot keep, or a risk with App Review, so a rewrite of the text keeps away from it.

- **No `RATP`, `IDFM` or `Île-de-France Mobilités` outside the source line.** The rules of App Review forbid the mark of others, in the title, the subtitle and the keywords above all, and the source line shows the data credit that the licence asks for and the sentence that TROME is not their app.
- **No promise of times for every line.** The source lists only the next three or four trains of a stop, and the times follow the service hours, so the text says "the next trains", never "live times for every line".
- **No promise of a start with no tap.** Version 1 has no silent start: a closed app sends a notification, and one tap brings the Live Activity.
  The first lines say what the rider gets, and the notification is named once, in the block "Quand les trains s’affichent" / "When the trains show".
  Only a real iPhone at a station shows that iOS wakes the closed app, so see the notification come on your iPhone before you paste the text; if it does not come, remove the closed-app sentences from the description, the notes and the TestFlight text.
- **No headline that says a notification pops up.** The notification is the way in of version 1, not what TROME is.
- **No claim outside Paris.** The app is not blocked elsewhere, but it shows nothing useful there, except the example, so the first lines name the limit.
- **No claim about the battery, and no `real time`.** Nobody measured the first, and the second promises more than the source gives.
