This page holds every text that you paste into App Store Connect and TestFlight for TROME 1.0, in French and in English, with the limit of its field and its size.
[The release guide](/en/maintain/release-on-the-app-store/) says where each field is and when to fill it in.
This page says what goes in it.

French comes first, because most riders are French: paste the French text in the primary language, then the English text.
Apple counts characters for every field below except the keywords and the review notes, which it counts in bytes: an accented letter such as é takes two bytes.
The limits are those of Apple's help pages, read on 7 October 2026: [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/) and [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/).

## The store title: three to try, in order

The title is the first thing a rider reads, and its limit is 30 characters.
The exact title `TROME` is probably refused: two apps already carry the word, a Peruvian newspaper and a hospital group in India, and App Store Connect refuses a name that another app holds.
So the title is `TROME`, a dash, and a few words that say what it is.
The name under the icon stays `TROME`, because it is another field that the store does not check.

Try the candidates in this order, and take the first that App Store Connect accepts.
Apple's public search (read on 7 October 2026) shows no app with these titles, and no transit app with the word.
It cannot see a name that an unreleased app record holds, so only App Store Connect settles it.

| Try | French (primary) | English (U.S.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TROME – Métro et RER Paris` (26) | `TROME – Paris Metro & RER` (25) |
| 2 | `TROME – Prochains trains Paris` (30) | `TROME – Next Paris Trains` (25) |
| 3 | `TROME – Prochains passages` (26) | `TROME – Next Metro & RER` (24) |

The first title says the two things that a Paris rider types in the search: the network and the city.
Other apps say the same in their titles, for example `Métro Paris & RER` and `Paris Metro, RER & Offline Map`, so no word of it belongs to one app, and the dash and `TROME` make the whole title yours.
The third title has no `RER` and no city: keep it for a refusal of a word.
The title never carries the name of RATP or of Île-de-France Mobilités: the source line at the end of the description is the only place for them.

## The subtitle and the promotional text: the first screen of the page

A rider who scrolls the store reads the title, the subtitle and the first screenshots, then the promotional text.
Both languages say the same facts: where TROME works, what it shows, and that it is free with no account and no ads.
The subtitle is a phrase on purpose, because Apple calls it one, and the promotional text is two full sentences.
The promotional text and the first sentence of the description show one after the other, so they say two things: the promotional text says who TROME is for and what the rider no longer has to do, and the first sentence of the description says what TROME is for.
The promotional text names the metro and the RER in a full sentence, because titles 2 and 3 have no `RER`, and whichever title App Store Connect accepts, the top of the store must still say it.
Neither names the way in: the block "Quand les trains s’affichent" / "When the trains show" tells it once.

The first sentence matters most, because it is the one that every rider reads.

```text wrap
I make TROME alone. It shows the next metro and RER trains at the station where you are, as a Live Activity on the lock screen. It works in Paris and Île-de-France only, so review it from anywhere with the example row below, which starts the Live Activity by hand; the attached video shows the start with the app closed, at a real station. No account, no sign-in, no purchase, no ads, no tracking. iPhone only, iOS 18 or later.

TO REVIEW FROM ANYWHERE (about 2 minutes, nothing to set up):
1. Open TROME. iOS asks for your location. After "Allow While Using App", it asks for "Always", then for notifications. After "Allow Once", it asks only for notifications, up to a minute later. After "Don't Allow", it asks neither. If the question does not show, tap Continue in the Location row. Any answer is fine: the example needs none of them. Outside Paris, the top card says "Nothing on the lock screen" and some rows are orange because a permission is missing. This is normal, and the example works.
2. On the main screen, tap "See an example". A Live Activity for Châtelet appears, with SAMPLE times made inside the app (no position, no network). Press the side button to lock the iPhone: it shows on the lock screen, and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one. The first time, iOS asks under it "Allow Live Activities from TROME?": tap Allow. If you tap Don't Allow, the top card says "Live Activities are off": tap Open Settings (or go to Settings > Apps > TROME), turn on Live Activities, then tap "See an example" again. Lock the iPhone again: iOS may then ask a second question under the Live Activity. Tap Always Allow.
3. The sample times count down while the app runs. If the lock screen says "Open the app", open TROME or tap "See an example" again: the sample starts again from that moment, for 5 more minutes. The example ends 10 minutes after you last tapped "See an example" or opened TROME.
4. On the lock screen, tap the station name to see the next metro or RER line of this station. The buttons 30 min, 1 h and Today snooze the automatic mode (on the example, they only end it).
5. On the main screen: the Automatic mode switch, the Snooze row, the Location and Notifications rows, "Privacy policy" and "Help and feedback" (they open web pages in Safari).

HOW IT WORKS IN REAL USE (I recorded the attached video at a Paris station, on a real iPhone):
- Within about 150 meters of a metro or RER station, with the app open, the Live Activity starts with real times.
- With the app closed, iOS wakes TROME near a station. TROME sends the station and its push-to-start token to my server, which starts the Live Activity through Apple's push service: it appears on the lock screen with no tap, and the server moves its times with updates on a broadcast channel of the station. This is why I ask for "Always" location: the app is closed when people arrive. With "While Using" only, the app still works while it is open.
- When the start cannot happen (no network, or the push is refused), TROME sends a local notification instead, and one tap starts the Live Activity. Notifications are optional, and only this fallback.
- The position is used on the iPhone to find the nearest station. It is never sent to my server. The server gets the identifier of a stop and, for the start only, the push-to-start token, which it passes to Apple and does not keep; it holds only a hash of it, for 45 minutes.

TRAIN DATA: real times come from the PRIM service of Île-de-France Mobilités, through my server, which is running during the review. Metro and RER do not run all night: outside service hours there are no real times, but the example works at any hour. When the server cannot be reached, the lock screen says "Times unavailable".
```

## What 1.0 says to riders, outside the store

The first version of an app has no **What's New** field.
The 1.0 lines are on the site, in the pages [Nouveautés](/fr/whats-new/) and [What's new](/en/whats-new/), and in `CHANGELOG.md`, and they are not pasted in the store.
From the next version on, the one source of the notes of a version is the note that each change writes in `ios/changes/`: `make release-notes` prints, from these notes, the What's New of each language, under Apple's 4,000 characters, and the same lines go to TestFlight, the What's new pages and `CHANGELOG.md`.

## What the text must not say

Each line below is a claim that the app cannot keep, or a risk with App Review, so a rewrite of the text keeps away from it.

- **No `RATP`, `IDFM` or `Île-de-France Mobilités` outside the source line.** The rules of App Review forbid the mark of others, in the title, the subtitle and the keywords above all, and the source line shows the data credit that the licence asks for and the sentence that TROME is not their app.
- **No promise of times for every line.** The source lists only the next three or four trains of a stop, and the times follow the service hours, so the text says "the next trains", never "live times for every line".
- **The start with no tap, said once.** With the app closed, the server starts the Live Activity with a push, and a notification comes only when it cannot.
  The first sentences say what the rider gets, and the way in is told once, in the block "Quand les trains s’affichent" / "When the trains show".
  Only a real iPhone at a station shows that the start comes, so see it on your iPhone with the build that you submit before you paste the text.
- **No mention of the notification in the description.** It is only the fallback of the start, not what TROME is: the review notes and the TestFlight text name it.
- **No claim outside Paris.** The app is not blocked elsewhere, but it shows nothing useful there, except the example, so the first lines name the limit.
- **No claim about the battery, and no `real time`.** Nobody measured the first, and the second promises more than the source gives.
