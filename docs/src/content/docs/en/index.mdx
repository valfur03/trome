You walk up to Saint-Paul, and your iPhone tells you: **“Next trains at Saint-Paul (Le Marais)”**.
Tap the notification.
The waiting time of the next trains shows on the lock screen, and it stays there while you go down to the platform.
When you walk away from the station, it goes away.

Nothing to look up in the app, and no account to create.
