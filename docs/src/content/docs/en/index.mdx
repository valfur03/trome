When you come near a metro or RER station in the Paris region, TROME shows its next trains on the lock screen, as a Live Activity: both directions of each line, and the wait for the next trains.
There is nothing to look up in the app, and no account to create.

<Screenshot src={liveActivity} alt="The lock screen of an iPhone, with the TROME Live Activity for Champ de Mars Tour Eiffel station at the bottom, with no train times yet, and its snooze buttons: 30 min, 1 h and Today." />

TROME is not on the App Store yet.
Until then, you can install it yourself with a Mac.
