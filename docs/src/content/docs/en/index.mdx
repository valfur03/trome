TROME finds the station when you are about 150 meters from it: no station to pick, no account to create.
The trains go away when you walk away.
The **Snooze** buttons below them stop TROME for 30 minutes, 1 hour or the rest of the day.

I am Valentin Furmanek, and I make TROME for my own metro and RER rides, and for other riders.

## How the trains reach your lock screen

They show as soon as you come near a station, with the app open or closed.
Nothing shows? [Help](/en/support/#nothing-comes-when-trome-is-closed) says what to check.
