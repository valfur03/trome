You walk up to Châtelet, and your iPhone tells you: **“Next trains at Châtelet”**.
Tap the notification.
The wait for the next trains shows on the lock screen, and it stays there while you go down to the platform.
When you walk away from the station, it goes away.

Nothing to look up in the app, and no account to create.

TROME is not on the App Store yet.
Until then, you can install it yourself with a Mac.
