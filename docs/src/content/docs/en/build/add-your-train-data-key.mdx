The app that you build shows no live train times until you give it a key.
The times come from PRIM, the open data service of Île-de-France Mobilités, and PRIM gives a free key to anyone with an account.
This page gets you a key, puts it where the app reads it, and shows live trains on your iPhone.

You need TROME on your iPhone, as [Install TROME on your iPhone](/en/build/install-on-your-iphone/) shows, and the iPhone with its cable.

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep your key secret">
  The key is yours only: never send it to anyone, and never put it in a screenshot, a message or a git commit.
  Git ignores the file where you put it, so a commit does not take it.
  The key goes inside the app that you build with the **Run** button, which is fine for your own iPhone: do not give that app to other people.
</Aside>

## Put the key in your app

<Checklist id="prim-key-iphone">
  <Steps>

  1. Create a free account on [prim.iledefrance-mobilites.fr](https://prim.iledefrance-mobilites.fr).

  2. In your account on PRIM, create an API key for the API **Prochains passages - requête unitaire**, and copy it.

  3. In Terminal, create the key file and open it in TextEdit:

     ```sh
     cd ~/trome
     cp ios/Config/Secrets.example.xcconfig ios/Config/Secrets.xcconfig
     open -e ios/Config/Secrets.xcconfig
     ```

     TextEdit shows a file with one line, `PRIM_API_KEY =`.

  4. Paste your key after `PRIM_API_KEY =`, with no quotes, then save (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>) and close the file.

     ```ini
     PRIM_API_KEY = 0123abcd
     ```

  5. Connect the iPhone and unlock it.
     Quit Xcode if it is open, then open the project again, so that Xcode reads the file:

     ```sh
     open ios/TROME.xcodeproj
     ```

  6. In Xcode, check the scheme **TROME** and your iPhone, then click **Run**.
     Xcode builds the app again, with your key inside, and the app opens on the iPhone.

  7. In the app, tap **Testing tools** at the top right, then **Next trains of a station**.
     At the bottom, the screen says **Train data key: set**.

  </Steps>
</Checklist>

The Live Activity now shows live trains.
PRIM limits the calls for each key and each day, and the app asks for new times every 30 seconds while it shows them: close the app and stop the Live Activity when you do not use them.
[The train guide](https://github.com/valfur03/trome/blob/main/ios/guide/trains.md#one-call-for-each-stop-area) counts the calls of each screen.

To use the key in the Simulator of your Mac instead, follow [The train data key](https://github.com/valfur03/trome/blob/main/ios/README.md#the-train-data-key-prim) in the developer guide.

## Try live trains at any station, without going there

The app that Xcode puts on your iPhone has a **Testing tools** screen, and the app of the App Store does not.
It moves your position to any station, so that you can see the Live Activity of that station, with live trains, without being there.

<Screenshot src={appTestingTools} alt="The Testing tools screen: the button Show the Live Activity now, the position used by the app, the button Use the real GPS, and the fake position choices." />

<Checklist id="prim-key-testing-tools">
  <Steps>

  1. In the app, tap **Testing tools** at the top right.
     Under **Fake position**, tap **Pick a station** and choose one.
     The section **Position used by the app** shows the station, and the main screen says **Fake position** in orange.

  2. Tap **Show the Live Activity now**, then lock the iPhone.
     The lock screen shows the Live Activity of the station, with live trains.

  3. When you are done, open **Testing tools** and tap **Use the real GPS**.
     The orange **Fake position** goes away.

  </Steps>
</Checklist>

[Try the app at any station, without going there](https://github.com/valfur03/trome/blob/main/ios/guide/testing-tools.md) in the developer guide shows the other tools: coordinates, an address, and the next trains of any station.
