With a free Apple ID, Xcode signs TROME for 7 days.
After that, the app does not open, and its icon stays on your iPhone.
This page installs it again for 7 more days, in about 5 minutes, and the best day to do it is a weekend, before the app stops.

You need the iPhone and its cable, and the Mac where you [installed TROME](/en/build/install-on-your-iphone/), with the folder `~/trome` still there.
You do not turn on Developer Mode or trust yourself again, and you do not delete the app first: it keeps its data and your choices.

<Checklist id="iphone-renew">
  <Steps>

  1. Connect the iPhone to the Mac with the cable, and unlock the iPhone.

  2. In Terminal, go to the folder of the code, create the Xcode project again, and open it:

     ```sh
     cd ~/trome
     make generate
     open ios/TROME.xcodeproj
     ```

     Run `make generate` each time, even when nothing changed: it keeps the project in step with the code.
     The last line of `make generate` shows your bundle IDs, and Xcode opens the project.

  3. At the top of the Xcode window, choose the scheme **TROME** and your iPhone.

  4. Click **Run**, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd>.
     The app opens on the iPhone, and it works for 7 more days.

  </Steps>
</Checklist>

To get the newest TROME at the same time, run `git pull` in `~/trome` before you create the project again.

When the iPhone says **Untrusted Developer** again, do its trust steps once more, in [Run the app on your iPhone](/en/build/install-on-your-iphone/#run-the-app-on-your-iphone).
When Xcode shows an error, look it up in [Fix a problem with the install](/en/build/fix-an-install-problem/).

<Aside type="note" title="Keep the same name">
  Renew with the same `TROME_BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX` each time.
  A new prefix registers 2 new App IDs, and a free Apple ID can register only 10 in 7 days ([why](/en/build/install-on-your-iphone/#choose-the-name-of-your-app)).
</Aside>
